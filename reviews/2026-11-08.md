## Prior work
Searches covered CoT and program voting, CoT/PAL selection, XoT, ScPO, negative data in math RL, and majority-vote rewards (TTRL). The tool quota ran out before I could check further, so the list below isn't exhaustive.

- **CoT+PAL mixed voting on GSM8K (including G3)**:
  - **Already known:** that CoT and program formats complement each other, and that agreement signals confidence.
    - XoT, "Plan, Verify and Switch" (Liu et al., EMNLP 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.14628), reports that CoT and Program-of-Thought complement each other on math, and switches between methods using verification.
    - Zhao et al., "Automatic Model Selection with LLMs for Reasoning" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14333; the agent already cites it), uses an LLM to choose between CoT and PAL.
    - Kabra et al., "Program-Aided Reasoners (better) Know What They Know" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09553), uses majority voting over CoT/PAL samples as a confidence estimate.
  - **Seems new:** a plain plurality vote over mixed samples on a 1.5B Q4 CPU model, the equal-cost curve, the cheap greedy-anchored variant, and the measured cross-format error-correlation numbers. I found no paper reporting the same-wrong-answer rates.
  - The notes don't overclaim novelty here, but they should cite XoT and Kabra et al.
- **Transfer to BBH**: XoT's abstract says it generalises to logical reasoning. I found nothing closer on this specific setup (BBH state tracking at 1.5B, with PAL abstention). The finding that PAL abstains where it fits badly, which breaks cheap mixes, looks like a useful practical result, but I couldn't check it against prior work.
- **Label-free DPO (m3-dpo)**:
  - ScPO (Prasad et al., ICML'25, https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04109) already does DPO on self-consistency votes. The COT-majority arm that worked is essentially a ScPO replication at 1.5B. That is useful, but not new.
  - TTRL (https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16084) uses majority-vote pseudo-labels as an RL reward.
  - Setlur et al., "RL on Incorrect Synthetic Data…" (NeurIPS 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14532), already argue that negative data beats positive-only SFT for math reasoning. That directly anticipates the "negatives were the missing signal" reading, and the agent's notes don't cite it.
  - The cross-format MIX label, the agent's claimed novelty, has not yet given a significant gain.
