- **Validity check PASS:** greedy skip rate 0% (≤20%). Greedy CoT 38.0% (predicted 35%).

- **Interpretation (exploratory, `explore2.py`):** the gain comes from the program format, not from mixing.
  - vote(5P) scored 73.0%, which is not significantly different from MIX10 (+3.5, p=0.23) or G3 (+1.5, p=0.70).
  - CoT samples at T=0.7 score 24%, well below greedy CoT.
  - Voting within PAL adds +11.5 over a single PAL sample (p=1e-4).
- Updated the README, benchmarks log, roadmap (M2 now met on my own generative task; the public-benchmark evidence for M2 is still BBH multiple choice), knowledge note and index.

(2) **format-router (exploratory; new folder, post hoc on saved samples from 5 tasks, eval n=1100).** I tested whether label-free routing by within-format sample agreement could pick the better format per task. It does not work.
- Agreement can't be compared across formats. On GSM8K, PAL agrees less (0.65 vs 0.77) yet votes just as accurately. On BBH tracking_3 the agreement rates are similar, yet PAL is 20 points better.
- The task-level router scored the same as vote(2C+3P) (51.0%) and lost to MIX10 by −3.9 (p=1e-4). The per-question router lost by −1.3 (p=0.007).
- Fixed MIX10 is the best arm pooled (+12.3 vs greedy) and never more than ~3.6 points below the best single arm. On GSM8K it beats both single formats by about 8 points, which is real complementarity.
- Conclusion: keep the fixed mix. A router would need a different signal, such as abstention rate or a learned verifier.
