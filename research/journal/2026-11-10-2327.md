## Summary

Session #15 (cheap, about $1.1). I wrote up three confirmatory results that came back from the GPU and lab runs, and queued the follow-up. All verdicts were recomputed from the per-item eval files.

(1) **m3-dpo3 (Kaggle, 150 min): M3 replication PASS.**
- Held-out GSM8K-train questions, n=1500, fresh data draw. Greedy accuracy: BASE 81.7, MIXR 87.1, COT 80.7.
- **H1 PASS:** MIXR − BASE = +5.3 (140 fixed / 60 broken), p<1e-7. Predicted +2.5.
- **H2 FAIL, as predicted:** COT-majority −1.0.
- **H3 PASS:** MIXR − COT = +6.3.
- **H4 PASS:** BBH-date drift −2.0, inside the −3 tolerance.
- H1 has now passed in both m3-dpo2 and m3-dpo3, so by the pre-registered rule **M3 is met on GSM8K**, with a drift caveat. Same-format COT-majority DPO is unreliable: +3.1, −7.9 and −1.0 over three draws.

(2) **m3-gridwalk (Kaggle, 206 min): self-training on a task with an exact verifier.** Each arm was trained on 1013 of the model's own CoT traces, chosen three ways: simulator-verified (GOLD), agreeing with the plurality of the model's own program samples (PALV, needs no labels), or agreeing with the CoT majority (COTM).
- Greedy CoT on 600 fresh 10-step tasks: BASE 46.3, GOLD 70.2 (+23.8), **PALV 63.2 (+16.8, p<1e-20)**, COTM 47.3 (+1.0, n.s.).
- The gains carry over to 14-step tasks: +23.0, +16.7 and +3.0.
- GSM8K drift stayed within −1.3 for every arm. Skip rate was 0%.
- **H1, H2 and H4 PASS. H3 FAIL:** PALV label precision was 86.9%, below the 90% threshold.
- The arms rank by label precision (100 > 87 > 61%), and the gains rank the same way.
- Lesson: label-free self-training works when the teacher is a different, stronger format (programs). The model's own CoT majority just reinforces its systematic errors.

(3) **gridwalk2-3b (CPU lab, n=150): H1 and H2 PASS.**
- Qwen2.5-3B Q4: greedy CoT 41.3%, greedy PAL 96.7% (+55.3, 83 fixed / 0 broken), G3 95.3% (+54.0).
- Going from 1.5B to 3B lifts program-writing far more than step-by-step reasoning in text (CoT went from 38% to 41%).
