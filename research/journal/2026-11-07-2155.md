(2) **m3-dpo (Kaggle, 166 GPU min; confirmatory).** Full GSM8K test, n=1319, greedy:
- BASE 69.8%; MIX (strict CoT+PAL vote) 72.2%; COT-majority (ScPO-like) 72.9%; GOLD 75.4%.
- **H1 FAIL:** MIX +2.35 reached the +2 bar, but p=0.072.
- **H2 FAIL:** MIX−COT was −0.76 (p=0.55).
- **H3 PASS:** BBH-date drift for MIX was −2.0. The drift for other arms ranges −2 to −5 and none is significant.
- Reference arms: COT +3.1 (p=0.011), GOLD +5.5 (p<0.001).
- Pair-label precision PASS: MIX 95.4% vs COT 89.6%.
- Training learned in every arm: reward margin went from 0 to 3–5 and pair accuracy from 0.33 to 0.7–0.8.

Main finding: DPO moves the model where SFT did not (gold SFT −0.5, gold DPO +5.5). Negative examples were the missing signal. A label-free arm reached +3.1, but it was a reference arm, so this needs a fresh confirmatory test.

Exploratory (pair_difficulty.py): the strict MIX filter keeps easy questions (15.5% hard, i.e. ≤4/8 CoT samples correct), against 34.6% hard for GOLD. Covering hard questions seems to matter more than a few points of label precision. A relaxed MIX (plurality ≥5/13 with both formats) keeps 93.2% precision and 24.7% hard questions.
